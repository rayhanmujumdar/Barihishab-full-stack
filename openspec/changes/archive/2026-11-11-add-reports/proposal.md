## Why

Owners can see a month's numbers only on the Distribution board and their own statement; there is no single document the family can read, print and keep for a month or a year (RPT-01, RPT-02, RPT-04, RPT-05). All the data now exists (entries, snapshots, ledgers, loans), so Reports is the read-only layer that turns it into final and draft reports, identical on screen, on paper and in a saved PDF.

## What Changes

Source: `docs/reports_section_PRD.md` (Reports PRD v1.0), main PRD §6.9 RPT-01…RPT-05, §8.1 (S9 `/reports/monthly`, `/reports/yearly`), §10 (`GET /reports/...`). Scope = **P1 + P2** (user decision 2026-11-11).

**P1**
- **Reports page** `/[lang]/reports/{monthly|yearly|rent-roll|expenses|funds}` with a header (house kicker, title "Reports"), tab row (scrollable on phones), a period picker per tab, and Print / PDF / CSV actions. Tab and period live in the URL; `/reports` opens **Monthly · latest closed month** (F1).
- **Monthly report** (F2, RPT-01, G4, G5, G7, G13, G14): title + Final/Draft status, summary line, income and expenses by category (count, amount, % of total, bar), expandable categories listing their entries with bills, Expenses (all) vs "of which paid from Reserve", staircase, owner table with totals, notes (reopened, reserve overridden, unpaid units), owner-statement links. Closed months read the close snapshot; open months are live and labelled Draft (RPT-04).
- **Yearly report** (F3, RPT-02, G1–G3, G8): own year picker (years with data), "{n} months closed, {m} draft", totals for closed months with "+ ৳X draft", 12-month income/expense bar chart (draft months hatched), month table (Income · Expenses (all) · of which from Reserve · Reserve kept · Deficit carried · Distributable · Total), top 5 expense categories, owner year table (share now + share-change note, opening 1 Jan, entitlement, paid out, advances, loan deductions, balance now, loan outstanding) with totals, owner-statement links.
- **Draft labelling** everywhere (F4): title tag, chart/table "Draft" markers, "+ draft" on totals, a diagonal **DRAFT — NOT FINAL** watermark on every printed page.
- **Print and PDF** (F5, F6, RPT-05 PDF): A4 print views for Monthly and Yearly (no chrome, black on white, repeated table headers, page header with house name/address/title/status, footer "Generated by {Name} on {date} · BariHisab"). **PDF = the same print view**, auto-opened with the document title set to the PRD file name (`BariHisab-Monthly-{YYYY-MM}[-DRAFT].pdf`, `BariHisab-Yearly-{YYYY}.pdf`) so the browser's "Save as PDF" produces it — the pattern the Owners statement already uses. The monthly print adds an **Entries** appendix.

**P2**
- **CSV export** (F7): Monthly = one row per entry; Yearly = month table + owner year table. Taka with 2 decimals, English headers, ISO dates, Status column. Served by the API with `format=csv`.
- **Comparisons** (F8, G9): Monthly income/expenses/distributable "vs {last month}" and "vs {same month last year}" with arrows and words; category rows show "▲ ৳X vs last month" when the change exceeds 20%.
- **Rent roll** (F9, G10): units × months grid for a year (paid amount, "—", or "Arrear in {month}"), per-month and per-unit totals, Expected column, collection %; a cell links to the entry.
- **Expense analysis** (F10, G12): any month range + optional categories → total (with Reserve part), by category, top 10 vendors, monthly trend per category, top 10 single expenses.
- **Fund accounts report** (F11, G11): per fund account for a month or a year — opening, income in, expenses out, payouts and advances out, loans out, loan repayments in, closing; closing as of today matches Settings › Fund accounts and the Home House Fund.
- **Charts on every tab** (user request 2026-11-11): Recharts bar charts — Monthly income/expense by category, Yearly 12-month income vs expenses, Rent roll collected vs expected, Expenses by category and by month, Fund accounts money in vs out — on screen and in the Monthly/Yearly print views.
- **Navigation**: "Reports" added to the sidebar/More sheet for everyone with `report.view`; the owner bottom-bar **Accounts** (হিসাব) cell now opens Reports instead of Owners (G16, main PRD §8.1).
- New permission `report.view` (Super Admin + Owner).

### Out of scope
- **F12 Archive** of closed-month PDFs and "Regenerate" (user chose print-view PDF; nothing is generated on the server, so there is nothing to archive). Can return with a server PDF engine.
- Server-generated PDF files (no PDF library, no headless browser) — see design D2.
- **P3**: F13 Excel workbook, multi-house consolidated report, July–June tax-year view.
- The monthly report **email** (Notifications section; PRD lists the email itself as out of scope).
- Fund **transfers** in/out (no transfer entity exists yet; the fund report has no transfer lines until it does).
- Owner statement itself (lives in Owners; Reports only links to it, RPT-03).
- Any editing — Reports never write.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `reports`: read models and `/api/v1/reports/*` endpoints — monthly, yearly, rent roll, expense analysis, fund accounts, comparisons, CSV — and the pure report formulas (category shares, yearly roll-up, closed vs draft totals, rent-roll cells, fund movements per period).
- `reports-ui`: the Reports page (header, tabs, pickers, five tab views), draft labelling, print views with watermark, PDF-by-print-view file names, CSV download, owner-statement links, skeleton, 360px behaviour, bilingual copy.

### Modified Capabilities
- `rbac`: role map gains `report.view` (Super Admin and Owner); key counts change 44 → 45 and 11 → 12.
- `app-shell`: sidebar/More list Reports; owner bottom-bar "Accounts" points at Reports, Owners moves to More when not among the first three.
